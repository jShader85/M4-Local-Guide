Wilmington Riverwalk

Wilmington, North Carolina

The waterfront is a good place to start.

Wilmington's Riverwalk follows the Cape Fear River through historic downtown. With wide river views and shops and restaurants nearby, it is a great introduction to the city. Come for a relaxed stroll, then stay to explore the streets just beyond the waterfront.

Things to do

Make an afternoon of it, or stop by for a short walk. A few ideas for your visit:

  • Stroll beside the river and watch boats pass.
  • Browse nearby local shops and art galleries.
  • Stop at a downtown cafe or enjoy a waterfront meal.
  • Bring a camera for river views and sunset photos.

Plan your visit

Where to go
Cape Fear riverfront
Historic downtown Wilmington, NC

Wear comfortable shoes and bring water and sun protection. Allow extra time to find downtown parking, and check posted parking rates. Before you go, check the city's page for Riverwalk information and notices.
